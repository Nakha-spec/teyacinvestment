About TEYAC

Built on expertise, integrity and practical delivery.

Zimbabwean-owned engineering capability serving industrial, commercial and infrastructure clients.

Our company

Engineering experience with small-business flexibility.

TEYAC Investments (Pvt) Ltd, trading as TEYAC Construction (Pvt) Ltd, is a wholly Zimbabwean-owned engineering company incorporated on 26 June 2012.

We are a fast-growing civil, electrical, mechanical and telecommunications engineering company, offering design and construction as well as repair and maintenance contracting to industrial, commercial and infrastructure clients across Zimbabwe and beyond. Whether it’s a single service call or a multi-million-dollar project, our structure gives clients big-business solutions with small-business flexibility, quick response and fast turnaround.

Our expertise is evidenced by the quality of our past work in civil construction, structural-steel fabrication and erection, and telecommunications installations, repairs and maintenance. We maintain a database of some of the most qualified and experienced personnel in the field, backed by a best-practice S.H.E policy that keeps our people, our clients’ communities and the environment safe.

Our services

Integrated expertise for demanding sites.

Design, construction, installation, repair and maintenance across three core business lines.

01

Civil & earthworks

From site preparation to finished infrastructure.

TEYAC provides civil and earthwork engineering for industrial, telecommunications and development projects.

Base-station construction

Construction and maintenance services for telecommunications base-station civils.

Dams & catchment works

Design, construction and maintenance of dams, including catchment calculations and capacity-holding assessments.

Roads

Geo surveys and navigation designs, construction, surfacing, repair and maintenance.

Housing developments

Project layouts, waterway drainage, sewerage reticulation and house construction.

Site works & earthmoving

Site hardening, bulk earthworks, site preparation, hard-stand areas and contract cartage.

Plant hire & disposal

Earthmoving equipment and plant hire, plus supply and disposal of fill material — including specialist environmental earth-moving for contaminated material.

02

Structural steel

Fabrication, erection and dismantling.

We undertake structural steel projects of any magnitude — structural design, erection and dismantling — tailored to each client’s requirements.

GSM communication towers

Erection of communication towers up to 80m in height, including civil works — hot-dip galvanised and painted to local and international aviation standards.

Floodlight towers

Erection of floodlight towers up to 30m, including civils and electricals, in 3-leg or 4-leg designs, all hot-dip galvanised for corrosion protection.

Palisade fencing & burglar screens

Installation of hot-dip galvanised and/or painted tubular or angular palisade fencing and burglar screens.

Storage & structural steelwork

Storage spheres, roof trusses, staircases and stair rails.

03

Telecommunications

Installation, upgrades and maintenance.

Our technical teams support wireless and fibre networks from site survey through installation, testing and maintenance.

BTS & RF installations

Upgrades and maintenance on BTS and RF installations, with specialist experience on hilltop and rooftop sites — cabinet assembly, shelter assembly, antenna mounting and RRU installations.

Microwave links

Installation and maintenance of microwave links in 1+0, 1+1, space-diversity and XPIC configurations, on NEC Pasolink V4 and Neo Pasolink equipment.

Optical fibre

Optical-fibre splicing and testing, including trenching, cable laying and local-authority liaison.

Site survey & planning

Site surveys, technical analysis, drive tests and upgrade planning for proposed network changes.
